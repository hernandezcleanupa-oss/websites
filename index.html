What's Included

Know exactly what you're getting

Basic Cleaning

  • Dust all reachable surfaces & furniture
  • Vacuum carpets & rugs
  • Sweep & mop hard floors
  • Clean & sanitize bathrooms (toilet, tub, shower, sink)
  • Wipe kitchen counters, sink & appliance exteriors
  • Clean mirrors & glass surfaces
  • Empty trash & replace liners
  • Make beds (linens left out)
Most thorough

Deep Cleaning

Everything in Basic, plus:

  • Hand-wipe baseboards & door frames
  • Clean light switches, outlets & door handles
  • Dust ceiling fans, vents & light fixtures
  • Scrub soap scum & hard-water buildup
  • Clean cabinet fronts & backsplash
  • Wipe window sills & blinds
  • Detail behind & under reachable furniture

Move-In / Move-Out

Everything in Deep, plus:

  • Inside all cabinets, drawers & closets
  • Inside empty refrigerator & oven
  • Inside microwave & dishwasher
  • Wall spot-cleaning
  • Ready for inspection or move-in day

Need something specific? Add extras like windows, garage, grout or appliance cleaning when you book.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. We bring all of the products and equipment needed for your cleaning.

Do you offer eco-friendly or pet-safe products?

Yes. Mention it in the notes when you book and we'll use eco-friendly products that are safe for kids and pets.

How does the recurring discount work?

Recurring basic and deep cleanings save 15% when booked weekly, 10% every two weeks, or 5% monthly. The discount is applied to your estimate automatically.

Is the online price final?

The online total is an estimate based on the size and options you choose. For the most accurate price, give us a call and we'll set up a walkthrough to see the place and give you a quote.

What if I'm not happy with my cleaning?

Let us know and we'll come back to take care of anything we missed. That's our satisfaction guarantee.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients give us a key, door code or lockbox. Just add the details in the notes when you book.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Hazel Green, AL and serve Huntsville, Madison, Meridianville, Harvest, Toney, New Market, Ardmore and Athens, AL, plus Fayetteville, Taft, Elora, Flintville, Petersburg and Lynchburg, TN. Not sure if you're in range? Give us a call.
